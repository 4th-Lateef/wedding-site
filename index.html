Our Story

How It All Began

We went to school together from 2015 to 2018, and back then, we were simply good friends with no strings attached… From 2018 to 2025, life happened and we completely lost touch. Then, in June 2025, our paths crossed again, and what started with reconnecting soon turned into our very first date. From that moment, everything just fell beautifully into place. From friends, to lovers, to now being engaged and preparing to spend the rest of our lives together! Funny how life can bring two people back together at just the right time. What started as a friendship years ago has blossomed into a love story we’ll cherish forever. Here’s to our forever! 🥂💍💕
